Welcome

Thanks so much for finding my little spot in the world.

After Five Founder is about a journey. I'm a software engineer and an ERP consultant, and after eight years of putting big systems into big businesses, I'm spending my evenings building tools for small ones.

Can I build the side hustle to end all side hustles? Can I find paying customers, and grow a following from nothing? Is AI all it's cracked up to be, and can I use it to build a team of one?

Follow along as I work with customers, friends and family to bring a bit of the 21st century to businesses that have been getting by without it — and, now and then, to start one from scratch.

Case study

Working with my mum

My first project is my mum's business. Twenty years of trading, run on Excel 2010 and Xero, and every bit of it works — which is exactly what makes it hard to change.

I'm writing it all down as we go. What we try, what helps, what turns out to be a waste of time, and what it costs in hours and in money. Including the parts where I get it wrong, because there are plenty of those.

If your business runs on spreadsheets and habit, you might recognise a bit of yourself here.

The app

GobyGO

Run the day-to-day from your phone

Somewhere along the way I got tired of writing about the problem and started building for it.

GobyGO keeps track of the work — the jobs, the quotes, the crew, and whether any of it made money. Your accountant still keeps the books; this looks after everything that happens before them. It's live with its first customer, and I'm building the rest out in the open.

About

I'm Emma

I always wanted to run my own business and be my own boss. Somewhere along the way I fell for small business — the hustle, the sacrifice, the good and the bad and the highs and the lows. I've watched businesses fail, watched them do better than anyone expected, and watched plenty just sit quietly in the middle.

I've also spent eight years on the other side of it, implementing Syspro and Dynamics. They're wonderful systems, and completely out of reach for most of the businesses that would benefit most from them.

So this is my attempt at the useful ten percent — tools that just work, and an honest account of building them. Because starting something, or changing how a business runs, is never a straight line.

Buckle up. This isn't Instagram cute — it's real life.
