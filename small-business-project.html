Madrin Merchandising, going phone-first.

The business

Madrin Merchandising is my mum's business — a field merchandising company with a team of reps spread across the country, out in stores keeping products looking their best.

The challenge

Every rep filled in their call reports on an Excel spreadsheet and emailed them in. Each one was then copied, by hand, into a master spreadsheet to work out hours — which were then re-typed into Xero to run payroll. There was no website, and the whole thing ran on spreadsheets and email. It worked, but it ate hours every week and left plenty of room for error.

What we did

We started by mapping the whole process, end to end. Then: a refreshed logo and Madrin's first-ever website — and GobyGO, software that lets the team complete their call reports on their phones, calculates their hours automatically, and exports straight to Xero for payroll. No more spreadsheets, no more email chains, no more copying figures into a master sheet by hand.

Why it matters

This is the quiet, manual admin that drains small businesses — capable people spending evenings copying spreadsheets instead of growing. If we can automate it for Madrin, it's proof the same is possible for thousands of others. That's the whole point of After Five Founder.

How it works

The same honest process, every time.

No two businesses are the same, but the approach is. Four steps, repeated — and written up as I go.

01

Look honestly

Start with where the business actually is today — not where a sales deck says it should be.

02

Find the leverage

Pick the one or two changes that save the most time or bring in the most customers. Park the rest.

03

Build it lean

Use the simplest tools that do the job. No enterprise software, no jargon, no lock-in.

04

Share the lesson

Write up what worked, what didn't, and what it cost — so the next small business can skip the mistakes.
