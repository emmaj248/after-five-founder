Hi, I’m Emma

Welcome to After Five.

Thanks for finding this little slice of the internet. I’m glad you’re here.

Small businesses are the backbone of the economy, but they don’t always have the time, budget or resources to invest in expensive technology, consultants and complicated systems.

The good news? The game is changing.

AI, social media and technology that was once reserved for big companies are becoming accessible to everyone. And used well, they can make running a small business a whole lot easier.

That’s where After Five comes in.

I’m working with small businesses to figure out what actually makes sense — optimizing what you already have, finding smarter ways to use the apps you’re already paying for, and introducing new tools when they genuinely make life easier.

No massive tech budget. No changing everything for the sake of it. No pretending every new AI tool is going to revolutionize your business.

Just practical ways to work smarter, spend better and get more bang for your buck.

— Emma
Who is this for?

The small business owners wearing all the hats.

You’re running the business, doing the work — and somehow you’re also the marketing department, IT support, finance team and everything in between.

You know technology could make things easier. But between AI, new apps and the next big thing, who has time to figure out what’s actually useful?

  • Maybe you’re trying to get your head around AI and wondering if you’re already too late.
  • Maybe you’re paying for five different apps and still doing half the work in Excel.
  • Or maybe you just keep thinking, surely there’s a better way to do this.

You’re in the right place.
