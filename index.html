Why I'm doing this

Small businesses shouldn't get left behind — just because they're priced out, or too busy to learn one more thing.

In an era of AI and Big Tech, the people who keep our high streets running have the least time and budget to keep up — and not many of us have the IT know-how and the real desire to help. So that's what I'm doing after hours: building Goby GO to make their days easier, and sharing everything I learn — the wins, the false starts, the first sale, the first thousand followers. I might not pull it off. That's the point of doing it in the open.
